Dearest Mama,
This is kind of a long letter, but you have had such a huge impact on my life. Everything I do, even regular things on a daily basis, is all because of you.
To this day, if I love to dance and choreograph dances, it is thanks to you. When I was little, you put me in ballet, tap, jazz, and Bharatanatyam. And while I wasn't the best student in these dance classes, you made sure that I performed in every shushubharathi function. If I can learn steps from a YouTube video, it is because the first time I ever did that was when you pushed Shwetha to teach me the Desi girl dance, and I found joy in learning dance through other choreographies rather than a “dance teacher.” If I was able to dance in Shruti's sangeet, it is because of all the times you pushed me to perform on stage when my stage fright was a huge obstacle.
For how active and sporty I am, that is also all thanks to you. You put so much effort into teaching me swimming, skating, cycling, table tennis, and forced Jai to teach me badminton. Even though I did not want to learn initially, you pushed me to keep practicing, and now, these are the things that give me the most joy. Whenever I am playing any sport, I always think of you, and it gives me great happiness that I get to have these in my life.
If I love to cook, that is also thanks to you. In the summer break after the 6th grade, you taught me how to make roti. I whined at first, but that was the start of me being independent for my food. You put me in so many cooking and baking classes, which helped me learn so many new recipes, and you were always so supportive of me baking. Even though I never knew if a recipe would turn out good, and the crazy mess that would be created in the kitchen, you always encouraged me to keep baking, and now I love to cook and bake.
Everyone in my friend group claims I am the best and safest driver. They say I am one of the few people that they feel comfortable closing their eyes with if I am driving. This is also thanks to you. You had more confidence in me than I had in myself when I first learned to drive. You ensured I was so comfortable with driving and accompanied me for countless driving practices. If I love to paint and do various crafts, that is also thanks to you. I remember all the arts classes you put me in, which exposed me to so many art forms. You have taught me so many things, and every day you keep pushing me to keep learning, keep growing, and work towards the best version of myself.
Biggest of all is that you're my best friend, Mama. You have been with me through the best times of my life and the worst. High school in the US was the worst thing ever, but the only reason I was able to get through it without losing a sense of myself is because of you. When no one gave me company, you did. When I was going through my bingeing period, you helped me overcome it. You supported me through it all and created a space with me where I could talk to you about any problem I was going through without the fear of any judgment. The biggest thing that kept me stable was that I knew that no matter what, I could always go to you for any help, guidance, or advice that I needed.
You know, the other day Arush and I were talking about during stressful or overwhelming times, what keeps us grounded and calm, that everything will be okay. For him, it is paneer butter masala—whenever he is feeling like that, he orders this, and after he finishes the meal, his mind is clear, and he is ready to tackle his problems. For me, it is talking to you. That's why I am so shocked when you say I am just calling you for the sake of it, when that is the exact opposite. To this day, any problem I have or anything I am feeling, you are the first person I want to call. Any accomplishment, any failure, any mistake, I want to tell you first.
Thank you for being you, being so open-minded, being so supportive, and loving me so much. You have led me to all the right paths I have taken in life. I love you so much, thank you for being the best mom and friend ever!

To a gift
